Dog Encyclopedia

Beagle

Size. Small to medium.

Personality. Curious, friendly, and guided by its nose.

Everyday care. Offer daily walks, scent games, and regular ear checks.

Golden Retriever

Size. Medium to large.

Personality. Gentle, sociable, and eager to play.

Everyday care. Provide exercise, companionship, and regular coat brushing.

Poodle

Size. Varies from toy to standard.

Personality. Alert, clever, and active.

Everyday care. Plan grooming, daily exercise, and short training games.
